💡 The Lesson

Phishing attacks succeed because they create trust, curiosity, urgency, or fear. A familiar-looking message is not proof that a link is safe. Cybercriminals often imitate teachers, schools, banks, gaming services, and social media platforms.

Always verify unexpected messages before clicking links, downloading files, or entering personal information.

🕵️ Your Cyber Safety Check

  1. Check the sender: Were you expecting the message?
  2. Check the link: Does the website address match the real source?
  3. Verify first: Ask your teacher through a trusted channel.
🔐 THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK. VERIFY BEFORE YOU TRUST.
